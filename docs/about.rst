About
=====

Why this exists
---------------

This package began with a question from lure fishing, a hobby of mine:
what colour does a lure turn out to be under water? Answering it led into
ocean optics, a field that also reaches into how aquatic animals see and
how much light is left for photosynthesis.

I am not a specialist in this field. If a feature would make the package
useful to you, or a result looks wrong, please open an issue. I may not be
able to respond quickly, but I will do what I can.

T. Ishibashi

Reporting a wrong number
------------------------

A coefficient that disagrees with a paper you have is the most useful
report this package can receive. DATA.md_ exists because several such
disagreements turned out to be defects in the literature rather than here.
Say which number, against which source, and which edition of it, in an
issue_.

How the package is built
------------------------

- DECISIONS.md_: why it is shaped the way it is, including what was
  considered and rejected.
- DATA.md_: what is known about each shipped table, and what is wrong with
  it.
- CONTRIBUTING.md_: what goes in, what it takes, and what a release may
  change.

.. _issue: https://github.com/tishibashi-cpu/jerlov/issues
